Description

An excellent opportunity to acquire a traditional detached two-storey residence occupying a substantial site in the heart of Kenmare town. Teach Aosta is superbly positioned on New Road, just 70 metres from Henry Street and within easy walking distance of all local amenities. The property occupies a generous site of approximately 0.245 acres, with approximately 21 metres of street frontage, side access and a large south-facing rear garden. The residence extends to approximately 103.8 sq. m. (1,117 sq. ft.), with accommodation comprising a living room, kitchen, four bedrooms, bathroom and guest WC. An attached garage extends to approximately 60 sq. m. (645 sq. ft.). While the property would benefit from modernisation, its sizeable town-centre site, extensive street frontage, side access and large rear garden offer excellent development potential, subject to the necessary planning permission.
